# Review: Improv Wisdom 🎭

**TL;DR:** The art of improv is already within you; you just have to show up!

**Thoughts:**  
A truly insightful look at improv from someone highly respected in the field (and from Stanford Improv!).

**[View on Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/182052.Improv_Wisdom)**

---

Below are some quotes that I especially liked from the book. I attempt to synthesize them with my own impressions and thoughts as well, though much of Madson's work speaks for herself.

> Understanding the power of yes is easy; practicing that acceptance and affirmation in daily life becomes our challenge.

## 💡 Key Maxims

### First Maxim: Say Yes

> Saying yes is an act of courage and optimism: it allows you to share control. Yes expands your world.

Through "saying yes" to the offers presented during the scene, you open the act to more possibilities than closing those roads off. It's better to have multiple paths to go down (rather than limiting our paths).

### Second Maxim: Don’t Prepare

> Substitute **attention** for preparation. Then you will be working in real time… You discover that you already have the answers.

Like one of our lessons learned, the path that we need to take can be found by looking back at all the things we've done before.

### Third Maxim: Just Show Up

> Players step onto the stage because that is where things are happening. They just show up. Then the magic begins.

### Fourth Maxim: Start Anywhere

> There’s no need to find the right starting place… begin with the most obvious thing, whatever is in front of you.

### Fifth Maxim: Be Average

> Giving up on perfection is the first step… Striving for an original idea can block access to the creative process.

Such an important lesson that helped ease the tension off.

### Sixth Maxim: Attention / Mindfulness

> Life _is_ attention… This moment happens only once. Treasure it.

### Seventh Maxim: Work With What You Have

> Wishing things were different simply wastes time.

### Eighth Maxim: Stay on Course

> There is meaning in everything we do, even small tasks.

### Ninth Maxim: Wake Up to the Gifts

> Never let an hour go by without giving credit to someone.

### Tenth Maxim: Make Mistakes, Please

> If you are not making mistakes, you are not improvising. Mistakes may actually be blessings.

### Eleventh Maxim: Act Now

> The essence of improvising is action. Act in order to discover what comes next.

### Twelfth Maxim: Take Care of Each Other

> Be someone’s guardian angel. Share control; don’t hog it. Kindness is essential during chaos or a crisis.

### Thirteen Maxim: Enjoy the Ride

> Find joy in whatever you are doing, including ordinary tasks. Play is essential to human growth.
