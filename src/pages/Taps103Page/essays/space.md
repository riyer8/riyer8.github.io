## Space Objects 🪐

Improv is known to have no objects (except for mere wooden chairs). Everything else you have to create yourself, in the scene, in the moment. It's no easy feat to make imaginary objects in space feel real to an audience. We refer to these as "space objects"

> space objects _(noun)_: mimed objects created and handled realistically by performers to build a scene's environment.

These space objects are the storytelling tools, making the scene _feel_ realistic. The physicality of embodying items and surfaces also creates room for creativity.
