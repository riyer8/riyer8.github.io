## Celebration of the Failures

During the first week, we were shown to celebrate our failures through The Circus Bow. This excerpt from Improv Wisdom sums up the idea quite nicely.

> Instead of shrinking and berating himself silently with, “Oh, no, I really blew it!” the clown turns to the crowd on one side and takes a magnificent bow with his hands extended and his arms high in the air, proclaiming “Ta-dah!” as if he had just pulled off a master stunt. He then turns to face the other side of the audience and repeats the bow, “Ta-dah!” Doing it in both directions allows him a 360-degree view of where he is.
>
> <div style="text-align: right; font-style: italic;">Improv Wisdom (pg. 106-109)</div>

The idea of ritualizing failure and reinforcing positive reactions creates a culture were failures and mistakes are rather redirections or offers that can take the audience through a new journey. It also adds comfort to the dynamics of the group, feeling comfortable in the ideas each person brings to the table, whether or not it was "good".
