## It's Ok to Be Obvious ✨

One of the challenges of on-stage performance is to seek validation from the audience. I'd believe that one of the greatest moments would be to have a standing ovation for the performance you gave, everyone being in awe of what they see. The true question is: where does this awe come from?

With this comes the pressure of _acting original_ under the stage lights. Improv seemed that way to me as well.

Coming into the class, I was fearful of this. I never thought of myself as someone who was unique, who could come up with creative ideas. And now, with improv, I thought I would have to come up with those ideas on-the-spot. Instantly. You can imagine that felt like a lot of pressure.

**One of the biggest unlocks for me was to let go of the pressure of being unique. Be obvious**

It's ok to let the obvious idea flow. In fact, it can feel relatable.
