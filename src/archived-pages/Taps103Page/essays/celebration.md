## Celebration of the Failures 🎉

During the first week, we were shown to celebrate our failures through The Circus Bow. This excerpt from [Improv Wisdom](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/182052.Improv_Wisdom) sums up the idea quite nicely.

> Instead of shrinking and berating himself silently with, “Oh, no, I really blew it!” the clown turns to the crowd on one side and takes a magnificent bow with his hands extended and his arms high in the air, proclaiming “Ta-dah!” as if he had just pulled off a master stunt. He then turns to face the other side of the audience and repeats the bow, “Ta-dah!” Doing it in both directions allows him a 360-degree view of where he is.
>
> <div style="text-align: right; font-style: italic; margin-bottom: 1em">Improv Wisdom (pg. 106-109)</div>

The idea of embracing failures openly and joyfully creates a culture of comfort. Instead of denoting it as something wrong, we turn towards it with curiosity. In fact, this allows us to pick up on redirections through a new journey.

Celebration also adds comfort to the dynamics of the group, allowing everyone to contribute to the scene formation. The ensemble becomes braver, lighter, and more creative together.
